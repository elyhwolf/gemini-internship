    💡 The Ultimate AI Detection Cheat Sheet

    Images & Visuals
    • Hands & Fingers: Count digits; inspect fingernail consistency and knuckle folds.
    • Background Crowds: Look at faces in the distance; AI often turns them into nightmarish smears.
    • Signage & Text: AI struggles with consistent letterforms, producing pseudo-Cyrillic gibberish.
    • Specular Symmetry: Reflections in water or glasses rarely align with the primary light source.
    Writing & Text
    • Corporate Hedging: Repetitive boilerplate phrases ("It's important to remember...", "A testament to...").
    • Uniform Sentence Length: AI maintains monotonous syllable cadence without spontaneous human pauses.
    • Lack of Specific Stakes: Generic high-level summaries with zero messy personal anecdotes or weird idioms.
